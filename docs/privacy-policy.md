# Privacy Policy — FaizApp

**Last updated: November 10, 2026**

FaizApp is an internal Telegram-based application designed to manage and monitor field operations, including photo reports, work results, comments, and employee performance.

This Privacy Policy explains what information FaizApp may collect and how it is used.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, FaizApp may collect:

- Telegram user ID;
- Telegram username and display name;
- phone number provided by the user during registration;
- submitted photographs;
- information about submitted reports;
- verification results;
- comments and remarks;
- work performance and plan-related data.

If a person who is not yet registered sends a photo to a FaizApp group or to the bot, FaizApp stores their Telegram user ID and display name as a pending registration so the photo is not lost and the person can be invited to register. Such a record has no role and is not used for statistics until an administrator approves it.

## 2. How We Use Information

Collected information is used exclusively for the operation of FaizApp and related internal business processes, including:

- registering and identifying employees;
- receiving and processing photo reports;
- tracking completed work;
- calculating performance against assigned plans;
- recording comments and rejected reports;
- sending notifications and reminders;
- generating operational and performance statistics.

## 3. Photo Reports

Photographs submitted to FaizApp are processed as part of the field-work reporting process.

In the current version FaizApp does not keep copies of the photographs. It downloads each photo only to calculate a SHA-256 hash and stores the hash together with the sender, the Telegram message ID, the caption and the time of receipt. The hash is used to identify duplicate submissions.

GPS or geolocation data is not intentionally used as part of the initial system.

## 4. Data Sharing

FaizApp does not sell or publicly distribute personal information.

Information may be accessible to authorized personnel who require it to perform their operational or administrative responsibilities.

## 5. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect stored information from unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, or loss.

Access to administrative functionality is restricted to authorized users.

## 6. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as necessary to support the operational and reporting requirements of the FaizApp project.

Specific retention periods may be defined as the system develops and its internal data-retention requirements are formalized.

## 7. User Responsibilities

Users should only submit information and photographs that are relevant to the intended field-work reporting process.

Users should not intentionally submit confidential information unrelated to their work reports.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as FaizApp develops and new functionality is introduced.

The latest version will be published at the official Privacy Policy URL.

## 9. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of information within FaizApp, please contact the person responsible for administration of the FaizApp system.
